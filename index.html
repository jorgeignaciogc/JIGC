Jorge I. González Cázares

Associate Researcher ‘C’ (Investigador Asociado ‘C’), IIMAS – Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México

My research centres on probability and stochastic processes, from the fluctuation theory, path geometry, and convex minorants of Lévy processes to the design and complexity analysis of exact and approximate Monte Carlo simulation algorithms. My interests include optimal Wasserstein couplings, limit theorems, Bayesian non-parametric survival analysis, the mathematical foundations of machine learning, and mathematical finance.

CLT & Convergence Rates Sep 2025 · 15:52

Multivariate CLT for Lévy processes: convergence rates without moment assumptions ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares, D. Kramer-Bang, and A. Mijatović (Bernoulli, 2026+)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

For a $d$-dimensional Lévy process $X_t$ with zero mean and unit covariance matrix $I_d$, the Central Limit Theorem holds as $t \to \infty$ ($t^{-1/2}X_t \xrightarrow{d} N_d(0,I_d)$). Without assuming $2+\delta$ moments, we prove that finite second moment is equivalent to the Dini-type integrability of the convex distance $d_c(t)$ under a modified jump-truncated covariance scaling $\sqrt{t}\Delta_t$:

$$\mathbb{E}\big[\|X_1\|^2\big] < \infty \iff \int_1^\infty d_c^{\mathrm{mod}}(t)\,\frac{dt}{t} < \infty.$$

In contrast, under the classical $\sqrt{t}$ scaling, $\int_1^\infty d_c(t)\,\frac{dt}{t} < \infty$ requires an extra $x^2 \log(1+x)$ moment.

Optimal Wasserstein Couplings Nov 2024 · 18:32

Asymptotically optimal Wasserstein couplings for the small-time stable domain of attraction ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares, D. Kramer-Bang, and A. Mijatović (Ann. Inst. H. Poincaré Probab. Statist., 2026+)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Studies the $q$-Wasserstein distance $\mathcal{W}_q(X_t / g(t), Z)$ ($q < \alpha$) between a multivariate Lévy process $X_t$ and its small-time $\alpha$-stable limit $Z$ as $t \downarrow 0$. Using thinning of Poisson point processes in the normal domain ($g(t)=t^{1/\alpha}$) and LePage series comonotonic couplings in the non-normal domain, we derive matching upper and lower bounds showing a sharp phase transition between polynomial rates $\mathcal{O}(t^{1-1/\alpha})$ and slower-than-logarithmic rates.

Exact Simulation · Part I & II Jun 2023 · 17:16 & 16:06

Fast exact simulation of the first-passage event of a subordinator (Parts I & II) ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares, F. Lin, and A. Mijatović (Stoch. Proc. Appl., 183, 2025)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Develops a fast exact simulation algorithm for the first-passage triplet $(\tau_C, Z_{\tau_C-}, Z_{\tau_C})$ of an infinite-activity subordinator $Z$ across a non-increasing boundary $t \mapsto C(t)$ by decomposing $Z = Y + Q$ into a truncated tempered stable subordinator $Y$ and a compound Poisson process $Q$. The computational complexity $\mathcal{T}$ satisfies $\mathbb{E}[e^{\theta \mathcal{T}}] < \infty$, yielding unbiased Monte Carlo solvers for generalized Caputo fractional parabolic PDEs.

Exact simulation of the first-passage event of a tempered stable subordinator & Monte Carlo applications ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares, F. Lin, and A. Mijatović (Stoch. Sys., 15(1): 50–87, 2024)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

In Part 1, I show how exact samples of the first-passage triplet $(\tau_B, S_{\tau_B-}, S_{\tau_B})$ across a non-increasing curve $B$ eliminate time-stepping bias in Feynman–Kac representations of fractional PDEs ($\partial_t^\alpha u = \mathcal{L}u$) and barrier option pricing. In Part 2, I explain the internal algorithm combining an Esscher exponential change of measure $\mathbb{P}_q$, adaptive barrier stepping, and Devroye's log-concave rejection sampler with $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-2})$ Monte Carlo complexity.

★ Presented by Jorge · Part 1 & 2 May 2022 · 17:23 & 21:31

When is the convex hull of a Lévy path smooth? (Parts 1 & 2) ↗

Authors: D. Bang, J. González Cázares, and A. Mijatović (Ann. Inst. H. Poincaré Probab. Statist., 60(4): 2352–2381, 2024)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

A corner in the convex minorant $C_t$ on $[0,T]$ corresponds to an isolated point in the random set of face slopes $\mathcal{S} = \{\xi_n / \ell_n : n \ge 1\}$. By the Stick-Breaking Representation Theorem, $\mathcal{S}' = \mathcal{L}(\mathcal{S})$ is a deterministic closed set. For infinite-variation processes, we establish the dichotomy between abrupt processes ($\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{S})=\emptyset$, no smooth points) and strongly eroded processes ($\mathcal{L}(\mathcal{S})=\mathbb{R}$, $C^1$-smooth at every non-vertex point) via the Fourier integral criterion:

$$S_1(r) = \int_{\mathbb{R}} \mathrm{Re}\!\left(\frac{1}{1+\Psi(u)-ir}\right) du = \infty.$$

★ Presented by Jorge · Monte Carlo Mar 2021 · 09:34

TSB-Algorithm: Monte Carlo algorithm for the extrema of tempered stable processes ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares and A. Mijatović (Adv. Appl. Probab., 1–28, 2023)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Combines the stick-breaking representation of the convex minorant with an Esscher measure change $\left.\frac{d\mathbb{P}}{d\widetilde{\mathbb{P}}}\right|_{\mathcal{F}_t} = e^{\eta(X_t - \gamma t) - c t}$ to reduce tempered stable (CGMY) extrema simulation to exact stable increment sampling. Achieves geometric bias decay and near-optimal $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-2})$ Monte Carlo complexity for barrier options and the Ulcer risk index.

Convex Minorants Jul 2022 · 11:03

Hölder continuity of the convex minorant of a Lévy process ↗

Authors: D. Kramer-Bang, J. González Cázares, and A. Mijatović (Electron. Commun. Probab., 28: 1–12, 2023)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Establishes a $0\text{–}1$ law for the $r$-Hölder continuity of the convex minorant $C$ on $(0,T)$ via the boundedness of the $r$-slopes $|\xi_n|/\ell_n^r$. The critical Hölder exponent is $r_{\mathrm{crit}} = 1/2$ if $\sigma^2 > 0$ and $r_{\mathrm{crit}} = 1/\beta$ otherwise, where $\beta \in [1,2]$ is the Blumenthal–Getoor index.

Stable Densities & Regularity Jul 2022 · 53:52

Joint density of the stable process and its supremum: regularity and upper bounds ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares, A. Kohatsu-Higa, and A. Mijatović (Bernoulli, 29(4): 3443–3469, 2023)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Proves for the first time that the joint density $f_{(X_1, \bar{X}_1)}(x,y)$ of an $\alpha$-stable process and its supremum is $C^\infty((-\infty,y)\times(0,\infty))$ and derives sharp upper bounds for all partial derivatives $\partial_x^n \partial_y^m f(x,y)$ by combining the geometrically convergent stick-breaking representation with the Chambers–Mallows–Stuck generator and multilevel interpolation.

How smooth can the convex hull of a Lévy path be? ↗

Authors: D. Kramer-Bang, J. González Cázares, and A. Mijatović (Electron. J. Probab., 29: 1–36, 2024)

Stick-Breaking Simulation Jun 2021 · 12:42

SB-Algorithm for the simulation of the extrema of Lévy processes ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares, A. Mijatović, and G. Uribe Bravo (Math. Oper. Res., 47(2): 1141–1168, 2022)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Introduces the Stick-Breaking (SB) algorithm to approximate $\chi_T = (X_T, \bar{X}_T, \tau_T)$ by sampling $n$ stick lengths $\ell_1, \dots, \ell_n$ on $[0,T]$ and conditionally exact increments $\xi_k \sim X_{\ell_k}$. The $L^p$-Wasserstein error decays geometrically as $2^{-n/p}$, yielding optimal multilevel Monte Carlo complexity $\mathcal{O}(\varepsilon^{-2})$.

Concave Majorants & CLTs Jun 2021 · 15:52

Asymptotic shape of the concave majorant of a Lévy process ↗

Authors: D. Bang, J. González Cázares, and A. Mijatović (Ann. Henri Lebesgue, 5: 779–811, 2022)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Analyzes the arc-length $\Upsilon_T = \sum_{n=1}^\infty \sqrt{\ell_n^2 + \xi_n^2}$ of the concave majorant on $[0,T]$. Since $\Upsilon_T - T = \sum_{n=1}^\infty (\sqrt{\ell_n^2 + \xi_n^2} - \ell_n)$, we establish joint scaling limits for $(\Upsilon_T - \mu(T), \bar{X}_T, X_T, \tau_T)$ as $T \to \infty$ (and $T \downarrow 0$) across both the finite-variance Gaussian regime and the $\alpha$-stable attraction regime.

Fluctuation Theory May 2021 · 17:57

Convex minorant and fluctuation theory of Lévy processes ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares and A. Mijatović (ALEA, 19(1): 983–999, 2022)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Gives a short, elementary geometric proof of the Stick-Breaking Representation Theorem (SBRT) for the convex minorant of a general Lévy process and derives the Wiener–Hopf factorization, Rogozin's regularity criterion, and Spitzer's identities directly from Poisson point process markings with intensity $t^{-1}\,dt\,\mathbb{P}(X_t \in dx)$ on $(0,\infty)\times\mathbb{R}$.

Gaussian Approximation & Drawdowns Nov 2020 · 13:05

SBG-Algorithm: stick-breaking Gaussian simulation of the extrema of Lévy processes ↗

Authors: J. González Cázares and A. Mijatović (Finance and Stochastics, 26(4): 671–732, 2022)

Mathematical Snippet & Key Result

Couples the Stick-Breaking (SB) representation with the Asmussen–Rosiński small-jump Gaussian approximation $X^\kappa$ (replacing jumps in $(-\kappa, \kappa)$ with scaled Brownian motion) to simulate the joint law of $(X_T, \bar{X}_T, \tau_T)$, the drawdown $Y_T = \bar{X}_T - X_T$, and its duration $D_T = T - \tau_T$ for general infinite-activity Lévy models, with explicit Wasserstein bounds and multilevel Monte Carlo estimators.
